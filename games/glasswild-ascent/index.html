Glasswild Ascent

A procedural 3D traversal expedition across three floating ecosystems. Recover four memory echoes per route, master momentum jumps and air dashes, activate checkpoints, and reach each summit beacon without surrendering to the void.

WebGL2 terrain, vegetation forms, atmosphere, echoes, moving platforms, character geometry, effects, and synthesized audio are created locally at runtime.
