Cinderline Velocity

Race through ordered reactor gates, outpace three signal rivals, and clear three circuits. Choose an unlocked course to launch.

An adaptive hover-racing campaign with WebGL2 3D and a playable Canvas2D track fallback. Cross numbered reactor gates in order and in the racing direction, manage drift charge, spend boost on the straights, and beat three increasingly technical Cinderline circuits. Race three deterministic signal rivals on each track: their lap progress and finishing positions now count. A first- or second-place finish is needed on every circuit for a ranked championship; slower finishes still unlock the next course for practice. Clearing a circuit unlocks the next; finished times are saved locally when browser storage permits.

Only a consecutive circuit 1 → 2 → 3 championship with first- or second-place finishes in every race submits a browser-local competitive score. The three rivals have independent, deterministic corner-aware pace; they do not collide or use networking. Individual clears and practice runs save local best times but are unranked.

WebGL2 draws the full 3D race. If WebGL2 is unavailable or lost, a readable top-down Canvas2D view still shows the real circuit, gates, opponents and moving craft. World geometry, racing physics and audio are generated locally; no external game art assets.
