Signal Choir

Listen and watch the choir. Repeat each signal in order. Only completed patterns keep their note points; mistakes remove unfinished notes and cost 120 points. P pauses; Resume continues the interrupted note without charging or losing your progress. Lost tab focus freezes playback; resume continues from the interrupted note without replay fees or lost progress.

Controller: pad 1 selected. D-pad or stick ←/→ to choose; A to sing. B replay (-90), Start pause/resume, X restart.
Guide & controls

Watch each numbered signal flash, then repeat the entire pattern with pads 1–5 or the number keys. Complete six rounds to synchronize the choir. Wrong notes cost one life, remove unfinished-round points, and deduct 120 points. You have three lives.

P pauses or resumes the current sequence; Replay costs 90 points but keeps already matched notes. R restarts. If the tab loses focus, playback pauses without a penalty. The game also uses optional synthesized tones; visual flashes carry the same playable sequence. Standard gamepad: D-pad or left stick left/right picks one of five notes, A sings once, B replays for 90 points, Start pauses or resumes, X restarts. Connect a controller and release held buttons once before playing.
