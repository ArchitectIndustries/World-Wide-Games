How to win

Each match lasts six rounds. Spend the round's energy to commit envoys from your hand into Harbor, Foundry, or Archive. You may play several cards if energy remains, then end the round and the rival delegation answers.

  • Win at least two of the three districts after round six.
  • A 1–1 split with one tied district is a deadlock; total power never overrides district majority.
  • Harbor grants +2 to the first card each side commits there.
  • Foundry grants +1 to envoys with base power 4 or more.
  • Archive grants +2 to envoys with base power 2 or less.
  • Card abilities can reinforce a district, grow over time, or reward diversity.

Beat all three delegations to seal the Parallax Accord.
