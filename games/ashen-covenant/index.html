Ashen Covenant · Controls and objective

Cross each approach, bind the three Ember Sigils, defeat the guardians, then enter the Lord Gate. Defeat all three Lords to earn a local ranked score.

Move with arrows or WASD, or use the four arrow buttons. LIGHT attacks with a short 0.22-second recovery; DODGE can still evade. HEAVY attacks with a 0.40-second recovery and higher stamina cost; SWAP changes weapon; INTERACT binds sigils, rests at a shrine or drinks a flask when applicable. RESET restarts the current encounter.

Opening HELP pauses combat. If the top strip reports that saving is unavailable, progress may be lost when this tab closes.
