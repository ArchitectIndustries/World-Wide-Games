Orbit Breaker · Objective

Destroy incoming asteroids for points and survive the escalating waves. Large violet asteroids take three hits. Your three shield points are lost only on direct ship collisions—letting an asteroid pass below the screen is merely a missed target and does not hurt you. After a hit, the ship gains a short invulnerability window so overlapping asteroids cannot drain multiple shields instantly.
