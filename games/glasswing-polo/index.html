Glasswing Polo · Match guide

Play to three goals per round. Move or dash to serve. Win all three Grand Circuit rounds for a ranked local result. Exhibition and two-player matches are unranked.

P1: WASD/arrow keys (outside 2P), Space dash, E wingdrive. P2 local: arrow keys, Enter dash, Right Shift wingdrive. Touch pads: arrows steer, DASH strikes, DRIVE activates when charged. P pauses; R restarts the current round or continues after a finished result.
