Guide & controls

Rescue six residents before 75 active seconds elapse. Move with arrows/WASD, use Space to spray fire and E to escort. Both the resident and rescuer must reach the gold safety circle; press E again to secure each resident. Controller: left stick, A to spray, B to rescue, Start to pause. Mobile: directional buttons, SPRAY, RESCUE. Amber dashed rings warn of new spot fires about 1.35 seconds before ignition; spray the ring to prevent it or move away. P pauses, R restarts. Leaving this tab suspends the shift. Only a completed six-person rescue earns a ranked record.
