A runtime-rendered tactical browser FPS built for instant play. Infiltrate three original operations, secure encrypted uplinks, survive response teams that remember your last visible location and investigate around cover, then reach extraction. Only clearing all three operations earns a local ranked Frontline score; individual uplinks, partial operations and failed attempts are unranked.

Standard controller supported from Deploy through all three operations. Release all buttons/sticks once to arm a newly connected controller; A deploys/continues and Y holds an uplink. Enemy amber sight marks signal a short targeting wind-up. Break line of sight to interrupt their aim; enemies search your last visible location instead of tracking you through walls. Pause or switching apps never advances combat.

No external art or audio assets. World geometry, silhouettes, effects and sound cues are generated at runtime.
