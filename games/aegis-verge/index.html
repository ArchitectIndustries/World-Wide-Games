Aegis Verge

A three-operation procedural 3D hover-combat campaign. Break hostile patrols, secure each Verge relay, manage hull and shield energy, then drive through extraction before the next frontier collapses.

World geometry, vehicles, projectiles, atmosphere, effects, and synthesized audio are generated locally at runtime. No external game-art assets are required.
