Skyvault Cartographer

A procedural 3D aerial exploration campaign across three sky regions. Fly a survey craft through shifting cloud vaults, scan five ordered beacons per route, ride thermals to recover boost energy, avoid terrain strikes, and reach the extraction gate with the cleanest flight record you can.

World geometry, craft visuals, atmospheric markers, effects, and synthesized audio are generated locally at runtime using the shared WorldWideGames 3D engine.
