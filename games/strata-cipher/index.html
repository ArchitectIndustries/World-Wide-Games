ARCHITECT INDUSTRIES · WORLDWIDEGAMES

Read the buried pattern before you dig. Recover three artifacts from each of six sites. Primary excavates the selected tile; Secondary surveys nearby strata. Numbers show how many fragments touch a tile. Fault pockets damage integrity. Losing all integrity collapses only the current site; retrying discards that failed attempt’s survey cost from the final expedition ranking.

Arrow keys/WASD move the selection. Use your configured Primary and Secondary action keys, or the on-screen controls. Select a tile directly by tapping the grid. The Selected Tile readout speaks row, column and only clues you have earned: uncharted, surveyed signal or excavated contents. I or Inspect repeats the selected tile without using a survey charge. R restarts the expedition. Survey, excavate and restart act once per press; directional movement can be held.
