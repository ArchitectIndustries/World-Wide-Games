RIFT RELAY 2.0

Escort the shared relay orb through six escalating arenas. The carrier moves the charge; the partner keeps the quantum link alive.

Solo LinkYou control cyan. An adaptive pink partner escorts, retrieves, and pulses threats.
Local DuoP1 uses WASD/custom controls. P2 uses Arrow keys. Space/Enter fire team pulses.
ObjectiveCarry the orb through four ordered rifts per arena without breaking the tether or shield.
CampaignSix authored arenas add mines, gravity wells, patrol shards, and sweep beams.

Objective & controls

Deliver the gold orb through all four violet gates in each arena. Keep both runners within the tether range while carrying. A hit, a broken tether, or an active sweep beam costs one team shield and drops the orb.

Solo: Arrow keys or WASD always move cyan; saved remaps also work. Local: P1 uses WASD/custom keys and P2 uses Arrow keys. Space / Enter emits a short-range defensive pulse. P pauses, R restarts the current arena, and 1–6 selects an unlocked arena.
