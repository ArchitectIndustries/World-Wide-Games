Defend the Garden

Protect all 20 lives through 10 waves. Choose Bloom (50 sun), Frost (70) or Spore (90), select one of eight nodes and plant or upgrade. Towers automatically attack. Start Wave sends enemies. Earn sun from kills and survived waves. A failed defense is unranked.

Compact touch: Tower cycles type; ◀ Node / Node ▶ pick a site; Plant places or upgrades. Wave, Speed and Pause work normally. The camera follows the selected site. Swipe horizontally across the upper HUD to read all statistics. Tap a node directly on the board when convenient.

Keyboard: ←/→ select node, ↑/↓ tower type, Space places or upgrades, E begins a wave, P pauses, T changes speed, R restarts, I opens this guide, Escape closes it. Your current node is ringed white. No hidden scoring shortcuts.
