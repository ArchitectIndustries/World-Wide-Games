# WorldWideGames Long-Form Audit

Date: 2026-10-03
Release: **v45 — Vector Shatter**

v45 adds a five-sector authored campaign rather than a one-screen arcade loop. Vector Shatter preserves progress signals through furthest-sector and campaign-clear records while retaining immediate browser play.

The existing long-form catalog remains protected by dedicated regressions, including Vanta Frontline, Ironlight Breach, Astral Menagerie, Polyforge Studio, Ashen Covenant, Verdant Echoes, Rune Depths, Aetherstead Colony, Mosslight Vale, Circuit Rush, Fluxward Conclave, and Pulsevine Parkour.

WorldWideGames will continue balancing small instant-play releases with larger games that can deepen across future production cycles. Historical long-form audit detail remains preserved in prior Git revisions.
