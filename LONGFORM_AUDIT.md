# WorldWideGames Long-Form Audit

Date: 2026-10-03
Release candidate: **v48 — Parallax Accord**
Owner/operator: Architect Industries

## v48 continuity

Parallax Accord is structured as a three-delegation campaign rather than a one-match card toy. Each delegation uses the same core rules but different lane preferences and evaluation pressure, successful match score carries into the next delegation, failed attempts restore the delegation-start checkpoint, and furthest-opponent/clear/sweep records persist locally.

The release gate completes all three delegations through the real result/continue flow and verifies cumulative score, campaign-complete telemetry, sweep milestones, and durable progression. Its district laws and six card abilities provide a foundation for future card pools, new delegations, alternate laws, and challenge modes without replacing the underlying game.

## Retained long-form coverage

Dedicated regressions remain green for Lattice Armada, Rift Relay 2.0, Vector Shatter, Vanta Frontline, Ironlight Breach, Astral Menagerie, Polyforge Studio, Ashen Covenant, Verdant Echoes, Rune Depths, Circuit Rush, Fluxward Conclave, and other previously validated campaigns.

## Next depth target

Parallax Accord should receive longer varied-draft balance runs with emphasis on Archive low-cost tempo, Architect scaling in crowded districts, Anchor comeback frequency, rival delegation differentiation, and whether six rounds create enough tactical counterplay without overstaying the match.
